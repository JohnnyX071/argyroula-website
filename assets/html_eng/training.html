Certified Training in Relating Theory

Understanding, Assessing and Managing Interpersonal Relationships

An evidence-based postgraduate training framework for healthcare and mental health professionals. Interpersonal relationships play a decisive role in psychological well-being, the therapeutic process, and overall human functioning. Relating Theory (Birtchnell, 1993/1996) supplies a comprehensive, empirically validated framework for understanding, assessing, and modifying relational patterns.

Throughout the program, participants acquire specialized knowledge and applied clinical competencies to identify and assess the relational configurations that shape both their clients’ outside relationships and the in-session therapeutic alliance—a quintessential catalyst for therapeutic change.

The program is administered through the Center of Continuing Education and Lifelong Learning (K.E.DI.VI.M.) of the Hellenic Mediterranean University (HMU), combining rigorous theoretical instruction with applied clinical practice.

Target Audience

This program is designed for health and mental health professionals seeking to elevate their diagnostic and therapeutic mastery of interpersonal dynamics:

Psychologists & Psychotherapists

Mental Health Counselors

Social Workers

Psychiatrists & Physicians

Special Educators & Teachers

Family & Group Clinicians

What You Will Gain

Theoretical Mastery: In-depth comprehension of relational dimensions and functional vs. dysfunctional relating styles.
Diagnostic & Assessment Skills: Administration, scoring, and clinical formulation of standardized inventories.
Relational Profiling: Visualizing client configurations on the Interpersonal Octagon.
Bridging Data with Clinical Practice: Connecting psychometric profiles directly to client goals and therapeutic planning.
Dual Relationship Perspective: Decoding patterns in outside relationships and observing how they emerge in the room.
Intervention Tailoring: Formulating individualized interventions addressing specific relational rigidities.
Therapeutic Alliance Enhancement: Recognizing therapist-client reciprocal dynamics and safeguarding the working alliance.
Broad Domain Application: Individual psychotherapy, couples therapy, family systems, and organizational settings.

Core Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the certification, practitioners will be proficient to:

  • Master the theoretical and empirical dynamics governing human relating systems.
  • Administer validated, psychometrically sound relationship assessment instruments with fidelity.
  • Interpret complex psychometric profiles and synthesize findings into actionable case formulations.
  • Design targeted therapeutic and counseling interventions grounded in relational profiling.
  • Harness assessment data to enrich clinical decision-making and measure progress.
  • Identify and navigate complex relational patterns that manifest within the therapeutic relationship.
  • Strengthen therapeutic alliance cohesion through mutual relational awareness.
  • Seamlessly integrate Relating Theory principles into diverse clinical and community practice contexts.

Program Director & Lead Trainer

Dr. Argyroula Kalaitzaki, Full Professor of Clinical Psychology at the Hellenic Mediterranean University, possesses specialized international credentials in Relating Theory (John Birtchnell, 1993/1996) and its psychometric measures. Her scholarly work has made pioneering international contributions to the validation, standardization, and implementation of these instruments across clinical populations.

Official Certification

Participants who successfully satisfy all program requirements are awarded an Official Certificate of Continuing Education from the K.E.DI.VI.M. of the Hellenic Mediterranean University.
