Specialized Scientific Services

Delivering high-standard psychological and psychometric assessment, professional training and certification, clinical supervision, and judicial expert witness evaluations. Services are grounded in internationally validated psychometric tools, cutting-edge theoretical models, and decades of clinical and academic expertise.

Validated & Standardized Tools Available Online & In-Person Rigorous Scientific Ethics
Clinical & Diagnostic

Psychometric Assessment

Comprehensive and multidimensional assessment of adult personality and psychopathology (MMPI-3), alongside systematic mapping of interpersonal relationship dynamics for individuals, couples, and family systems (PROQ3, CREOQ3, FMIQ, PPROQ3-SF).

  • Personality & Psychopathology Assessment (MMPI-3)
  • Individual Interpersonal Relating (PROQ3 / PPROQ3-SF)
  • Couple Relationship Assessment (CREOQ3)
  • Family Relationship Assessment (FMIQ)
  • Comprehensive analytical report & feedback session
Learn More
Certified Education

Training / Certification

Certified continuing education in Relating Theory through the Center of Continuing Education and Lifelong Learning (K.E.DI.VI.M.) of the Hellenic Mediterranean University (HMU), designed for healthcare and mental health professionals.

  • Understanding, assessing & managing relationships
  • Mapping patterns onto the Interpersonal Octagon
  • Application to therapeutic alliance & relationship
  • Design of individualized clinical interventions
  • Official Continuing Education Certificate (HMU)
Learn More
Clinical Guidance

Professional Supervision

Specialized scientific guidance and supervision in administering, scoring, interpreting, and applying interpersonal relationship assessment instruments in counseling and psychotherapy practice, exclusively for trained professionals.

  • Case presentation and clinical formulation
  • In-depth scoring analysis & graphic mapping
  • Bridging assessment findings with treatment plans
  • Navigating complex interpersonal dynamics
  • Individual or group formats, online or in-person
Learn More
Forensic Psychology

Expert Witness / Forensic Reports

Scientifically documented expert reports, technical witness statements, and psychological evaluations for civil and criminal court cases, utilizing internationally recognized psychometric instruments with strict ethical standards.

  • Child custody, parental competence & suitability
  • Intimate partner & domestic violence / abuse cases
  • Evaluation of trauma, PTSD & psychological damages
  • Legal capacity & competence to participate in trials
  • Civil disputes & high-conflict family dynamics

About Me

I am a Full Professor of Clinical Psychology in the Department of Social Work at Hellenic Mediterranean University (HMU). I am leading the HMU's Gender Equality and Combating Discrimination Committee, and I am a member of the Quality-of-Life Lab, the Steering Committee and the Programme Committee of the postgraduate program "Interdisciplinary Management of Chronic Diseases, Disability, and Ageing".
