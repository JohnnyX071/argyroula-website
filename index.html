Hello, I’m Dr. Argyroula Kalaitzaki

I am a Full Professor of Clinical Psychology, Department of Social Work at the Hellenic Mediterranean University (HMU). I am also a member of the Laboratory of Interdisciplinary Approaches for the Enhancement of Quality of Life (Quality of Life Lab; QoL), a member of both the Steering Committee and the Programme Committee of the postgraduate program “Interdisciplinary Management of Chronic Diseases, Disability, and Ageing”, and I am leading the HMU’s Gender Equality and Combating Discrimination Committee. My scholarly interests, in light of both psychopathology and Positive Psychology, focus on:

  1. Interpersonal relationships
  2. Older people, dementia, and Alzheimer's disease
  3. Traumatic experiences
  4. Health Literacy

About Me

I am a Full Professor of Clinical Psychology in the Department of Social Work at Hellenic Mediterranean University (HMU).
